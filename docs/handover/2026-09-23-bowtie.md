## Goal of this thread
Make the corridor bowtie repair general (not Pasir Gudang specific): one call, `civil3d_corridor bowtie_fix`, that solves the
valley line of a bend, isolates the clash range (over one or two regions), swaps in a clip assembly, maps
ClipTarget + ClipElev, rebuilds, checks, and rolls back cleanly on failure. `bowtie_unfix` undoes a repair.

Decisions by Amir:
- Level move is spread from the hinge over every slope and bench (clip v0.4, "Spread From Hinge" = Yes), BUT the design
  ratio is the maximum: steeper than designed, or a bench whose fall reverses, is flagged for remedial protection.
- Valley level rule: `no_steeper` (default; cut side only lowered, fill side only raised) with `mean` as an option; where no
  such level exists the mean is used and flagged.
- Junctions are out of scope (separate topic, Amir's own workflow).
